Stress Management Tips

Personalized strategies to help you navigate academic pressure and maintain mental wellness.

Low Stress Tips

Maintain a regular sleep schedule (7-9 hours).

Continue regular physical activity or sports.

Keep up your healthy eating and hydration habits.

Balance study time with hobbies you enjoy.

Recommended for scores 0-10

Moderate Stress Tips

Practice deep breathing exercises for 5 minutes daily.

Use time management tools like the Pomodoro technique.

Take short, frequent study breaks to refresh your mind.

Listen to calming music while studying or relaxing.

Recommended for scores 11-20

High Stress Tips

Try guided meditation or mindfulness apps.

Talk to friends, family, or a trusted mentor about your feelings.

Reach out to university counseling or support services.

Prioritize relaxation techniques like progressive muscle relaxation.

Recommended for scores 21-30
